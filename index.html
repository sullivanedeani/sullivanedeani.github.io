Sullivan Kosorochi Edeani

I am a PhD student in Political Science at the University of Kentucky, specializing in International Relations and Comparative Politics.

My research examines interstate conflict, maritime security, strategic vulnerability, and the domestic political consequences of violence. I am particularly interested in how the geographic context of conflict shapes state behavior, political institutions, and citizens’ relationships with government.

My ongoing projects examine farmer–herder violence and institutional trust in Nigeria, geographic domains of interstate conflict and state repression, patterns of conflict escalation, political representation in the Nigerian federal civil service, and contentious demands and political institutions.

Born and raised in Enugu, Nigeria, I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Communication from Enugu State University of Science and Technology, where I graduated summa cum laude, and a Master’s degree in International Relations from Tokyo International University, where I graduated with Distinction as a recipient of the prestigious Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship.

Outside academia, I enjoy following football (soccer) and am an avid supporter of Chelsea Football Club.
